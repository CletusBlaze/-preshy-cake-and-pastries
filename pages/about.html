Our Story

Born From a Love of Baking

Preshy Cakes & Pastries started from a simple passion — the joy of creating something beautiful and delicious from scratch. What began in a small kitchen has grown into a trusted name in luxury baking and event catering.

We believe every celebration deserves an extraordinary centrepiece. Whether it's an elegant 5-tier wedding cake or a perfectly arranged small chops platter, we pour our heart into every detail.

Our commitment to quality means we use only premium ingredients — real butter, fresh cream, the finest chocolate, and seasonal fruits — ensuring every bite is exceptional.
