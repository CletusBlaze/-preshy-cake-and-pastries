FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?

For wedding cakes, we recommend at least 2-4 weeks. Birthday cakes can be done in 3-5 days. Small chops and catering need at least 1 week notice.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We deliver across Benin City. Free delivery for orders above ?50,000. Setup at venue is included for wedding cakes and catering packages.

Can I request a tasting session?

Absolutely! Tasting sessions are free for wedding cake orders. For other cakes, a small fee applies which is deducted from your final order.

What flavours do you offer?

Vanilla, Chocolate, Red Velvet, Carrot, Lemon, Strawberry, Coconut, Coffee, Marble, Fruit Cake, and more. Custom flavours available on request.

Do you cater for corporate events?

Yes! We provide full dessert catering for corporate events, product launches, and office parties. We handle everything from setup to presentation.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 7+ days before the event get a full refund. 3-6 days get 50% refund. Less than 3 days, the deposit is non-refundable.
